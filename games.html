"SIUSIUBOOM"

This is a first-person action game where the player takes on the role of a toy ammo crate. With the help of toy soldiers, the player must launch themselves toward the frontlines to provide support. But be careful — falling to the ground could alert the human owner, and that’s something you want to avoid…

"UNDER THE SNOWMAN"

by LiamXia, silvia, spyfox16, NikkiTian, russe111

Under the Snowman is a 2D side-scrolling crime mystery game about memory, guilt, and the things people choose to forget. The player plays as Mei, a college student who survived a traumatic incident on campus one year ago. Although she was not directly harmed, what she witnessed shattered her mind, erasing an entire section of her memory. Guided by vague clues and unsettling encounters, she begins to search for the truth about who she is and what happened in that accident.
